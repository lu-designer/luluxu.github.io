Pratthanadee Foundation

Industry: NGO

Services: Storytelling, Filmmaking & Editing

Date: July 2025 – Oct 2025

Documentary Social Impact

A story-driven video capturing the mission of a Bangkok-based NGO supporting marginalised women and girls through education.

Overview

This project was created for Pratthanadee Foundation, a Thai NGO dedicated to empowering marginalised women and young girls through education and mentorship. The goal was to craft a story-based video that communicates the organisation’s heart — not just what they do, but why it matters — in a way that feels genuine, emotional, and human.

Process

I was drawn to Pratthanadee Foundation’s mission of empowering women through education — a message that deeply aligned with my own values. From the start, I wanted the film to capture not just their work, but the human spirit behind it.

I interviewed the women and staff, filming within the school’s daily rhythm to capture genuine moments of learning, care, and connection. A voice-over helped weave these moments into a cohesive and heartfelt story. Shaping the story through the lens of an international volunteer, I aimed to reflect the warmth and purpose of the foundation. Through colour, sound, and pacing, I focused on evoking a sense of hope and dignity that mirrors their mission.

Impact

The video was shared across Pratthanadee Foundation’s online channels to raise awareness and support for their programs. It highlights the transformative impact of education and the foundation’s commitment to creating opportunities for women to build better futures.

Your support means so much and helps us share the voices and dreams of women and girls at Pratthanadee Foundation.

- Pratthanadee Foundation
