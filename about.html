ABOUT ME

I’m Lulu — a creative designer and storyteller who brings together design strategy, systems thinking, and visual storytelling to create experiences that feel both human and purposeful. Based in Sydney, I work remotely with teams around the world.

My work spans digital design, systems thinking, and creative storytelling — from designing & developing intuitive digital solutions, conducting in-depth user research to creating service communication tools, and crafting short documentaries that give a human voice to complex social issues.

Across every project, my approach stays the same: listen deeply, translate insights into clear visual narratives, and design experiences that feel meaningful and human.
