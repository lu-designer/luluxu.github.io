I design products that make complex concepts feel simple.

I'm a product designer who works end-to-end. My background spans UX research, service design, interaction design, and front-end development — which means I can take a product from ambiguous brief to tested, buildable solution without losing the thread.

My work has consistently centred on people navigating complex or vulnerable moments. At Macquarie University, I led experience design across two initiatives — redesigning student onboarding journeys and designing a mobile app to support student belonging and community connection during the pandemic. I've designed for mental health and wellbeing independently, including Gently, a self-initiated mobile app concept supporting creative solopreneurs through motivation and mental load, and a website for a holistic health practitioner grounded in spiritual and emotional wellbeing. Across these projects, the through-line has been the same: understand what people actually need, design systems that support them, and build something that works.

I hold a Master of Interaction Design (University of Sydney) and a Master of Social Work (Flinders University) — a combination that shapes how I approach problems. I don't just design interfaces; I think about the human systems, the edge cases, and the people who are hardest to reach.
