Films with Purpose: The Human Lens

In the past few years, I’ve been on a personal mission to travel the world and document human experiences in all their richness. That journey led to the creation of my YouTube channel: Little Lu’s Big Adventure — a passion project where I capture not only breathtaking landscapes but also the incredible stories of people, their cultures, and the traditions that shape how they live.

Now, I’m taking what I’ve learned through that storytelling journey and offering it to organizations with a mission: those working to support marginalized communities and amplify unheard voices.

I help NGOs, social organizations, and public services communicate their work and impact through emotionally resonant, people-centered videos.

What I offer

  • Short documentaries and campaign videos
  • Staff and community interviews (filming + editing + scripting)
  • Social-media-ready video formats

Behind the Scenes

What moved me most during this trip was witnessing the quiet resilience and deep spirituality of everyday Tibetan life. Whether it was the early morning prayers, the warmth of strangers, or the unexpected encounters along the way, the experience reminded me of the power of stillness, faith, and connection — things often overlooked in our fast-paced world.

Reach & Impact

This video is part of an ongoing series exploring Tibetan culture and spirituality. It has reached hundreds of viewers on YouTube and sparked thoughtful engagement from people curious about life beyond the tourist lens. Many shared that it offered them a more grounded and intimate glimpse into the life in Tibet.
